When it makes sense to call us

  • Cost per kilo went up and you need to reformulate without the customer noticing.
  • A new labelling rule, an audit or an export buyer arrives and the product is not ready.

What a meat processing consultant actually fixes

A meat plant’s problem is rarely flavour. It is control: real weight loss is not measured, cost per kilo is worked out on the kilo that comes in rather than the kilo that is invoiced, and defects are corrected by eye, batch after batch. The work covers five fronts:

  • Yield and weight loss. The first deliverable is a stage-by-stage mass balance, from the kilo received to the kilo invoiced. That is where money leaves without anyone seeing it.
  • Recurring defects. The emulsion that breaks, the ham that purges water, the salami that will not acidify, the cured colour that never forms. The diagnosis page lists the 24 costliest symptoms and the data we ask for each one.
  • Reformulation for cost or for regulation. Lower cost per kilo without the customer noticing, or reduce sodium without losing bind or microbial safety.
  • Food safety and paperwork. A HACCP plan with real critical limits, shelf life validated by trial, technical sheets and the registration dossier.
  • New markets. Export compliance and halal production, which is not «dropping pork» but a whole traceable chain.

Frequently asked

What people ask before they start.

How much does meat processing consultancy cost?

There are two entry doors at a closed price: a one-off technical enquiry, USD 300, for one concrete problem answered in writing within 48 working hours, and — for products sold in Colombia — the label and ultra-processed warning review, USD 290 for the first product. The yield and cost-per-kilo diagnosis costs USD 3,900: three weeks, no travel, and whatever you paid at either door is credited if you book it within 30 days. Programmes — recovering yield, reformulating against a regulation, qualifying the plant for export — start at USD 12,000 and are quoted only after the diagnosis. Laboratory tests, official fees and certification bodies are billed separately.

Do you work with plants outside Latin America?

Yes. The work is remote and built on your production records, so location matters less than data. We deliver in writing in English or Spanish; if the case needs someone on the floor, plant days are quoted as a separate line.

Will you publish our formulation?

No. The technical sheets on this site explain the phenomenon, the criteria and the risk, never a client’s formula. Your formulation, dosages and process curves are worked out with your team and stay in your plant.

Can you get us HACCP or halal certification?

We prepare the plant, the HACCP plan with real critical limits, the validated shelf life and the dossier so that you pass the audit the first time. The certificate itself is issued by the authority or the accredited body, and we do not promise their timelines.

What data do you need for the diagnosis?

Input and output weights per batch and per stage, current formulations, process temperatures, and returns and complaints from recent months. With that we build the yield balance; without it, any consultant is only offering an opinion.
